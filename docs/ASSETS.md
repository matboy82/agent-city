# Asset provenance

The curated furniture registry is preserved from the local Crew OS experiment and expanded with five selected CC0 Poly Pizza Office Pack models. The selected models and their creators were checked against the experiment's per-model attribution manifest before copying; their source and author appear in the in-app Credits view. Kenney Furniture Kit and Space Kit are also CC0:

- https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
- https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit
- https://poly.pizza/bundle/Office-Pack-UGIy7YcQP9

Supplied Jeff otter portrait and Relay portrait are copied unchanged from the experiment. They are not synthesized from another portrait. Existing prototype GLB bodies are preserved as application assets. They are not newly commissioned production character art. Jefferson and Jev have neutral initials where no supplied portrait is available.

Only selected models ship in `public/assets`; the much larger unreviewed/staged packs are excluded. New avatars require reviewed application assets. Office proposals select stable catalog IDs and bounded slots; they cannot introduce arbitrary URLs, model files, shaders, or scripts.

The city uses imported space structures, planted grounds, walkways, signage and state beacons. Offices use catalog-backed furniture, fixed anchors, live screens and camera-aware walls. A failed model reports its asset ID in diagnostics; all operational controls remain available.
